Fix Everything Triage: label Omarchy issues

Fix Everything Triage sorts Omarchy's open issues into queues — look now, ready for an agent, upstream, ask the reporter, park — with TypeSafe's Jev model making the judgment calls. It is read-only: it never labels, comments on or closes anything. Whether it sorts well can only be checked against people's judgment of the same issues, so here are 80 open issues drawn at random across ages, lengths and labels. Label as many as you like, even one, then press copy my labels for a comment and paste the result as a reply on the Fix Everything discussion in the Omarchy repository.
